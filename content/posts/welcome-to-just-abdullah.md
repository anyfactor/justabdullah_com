Welcome. I'm Abdullah, a PhD Researcher at Memorial University of Newfoundland. I live in St John's, Newfoundland and Labrador.

This site covers the research questions that take up most of my time:

- **Synthetic data and LLM-generated data**: how we generate, validate, and trust data that no longer comes directly from the world.
- **Information systems and design science**: building and evaluating artifacts that solve real organizational problems.
- **Human-computer interaction and explainable AI**: making intelligent systems understandable to the people who rely on them.
- **Data management**: the unglamorous but essential foundation everything above depends on.

Before starting my PhD, I worked in three roles. I was a Developer Relations Engineer in the Data-as-a-Service space. I was a Senior Executive in risk management and treasury investments at a national stock exchange regulator. I was a freelance software engineer working with data and Python.

Expect a mix of research notes, literature summaries, and the occasional engineering write-up. Thanks for stopping by.
